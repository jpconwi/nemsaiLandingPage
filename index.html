Meet NEMSAI

NEMSAI is the chat assistant of NEMSU Tandag Main Campus. Ask it a question the way you would ask a person at the registrar's desk, and it answers from the campus's own information.

  • Ask about the campus Programs, offices, news, announcements and where to find a building or gate on the map.
  • Check rooms and instructors See which rooms are free and whether an instructor is in school today.
  • Report a problem Send a concern, report or feedback about a broken fan, a leaking faucet or anything else on campus, and follow its status.
  • Reserve a facility Request a room or facility. An administrator approves it before it is confirmed.
