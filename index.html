Meet NEMSAI

NEMSAI is the chat assistant of NEMSU Tandag Main Campus. Ask a question the way you would ask someone at the registrar's desk, and get answers from campus information.

✓  What can NEMSAI do?

⌂
Ask about the campusPrograms, offices, news, and where to find a building.
⚒
Report a problemSend a concern and follow its status.
▦
Check rooms and instructorsSee which rooms are free and whether an instructor is in school.
▣
Reserve a facilityRequest a room or facility.
