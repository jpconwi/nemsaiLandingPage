NEMSAI

The NEMSU Tandag Main Campus assistant

Meet NEMSAI

NEMSAI is the chat assistant of NEMSU Tandag Main Campus. Ask it a question the way you would ask a person at the registrar’s desk, and it answers from the campus’s own information.

  • Ask about the campusPrograms, offices, news, announcements and where to find a building or gate on the map.
  • Check rooms and instructorsSee which rooms are free and whether an instructor is in school today.
  • Report a problemSend a concern, report or feedback about a broken fan, a leaking faucet or anything else on campus, and follow its status.
  • Reserve a facilityRequest a room or facility. An administrator approves it before it is confirmed.

Where do you want to use NEMSAI?

All three use the same NEMSU data. Pick the one that suits your device.

  • NEMSAI Web

    The full website for phones and computers: chat, reports, concerns, reservations and the admin dashboard.

    Open the web app

  • Android app Suggested for your phone

    Download the NEMSAI app for Android phones from Google Drive and install it.

    Download from Google Drive

  • NEMSAI on Streamlit

    A lighter version that runs on Streamlit Cloud. Useful if the main website is slow or busy.

    Open on Streamlit

Sign in with your official NEMSU Google account (@nemsu.edu.ph) in every version. Google handles the sign-in, so NEMSAI never sees your password.
